Frequently asked questions

Does it take screenshots of my screen?

No. Zeitblick only reads window metadata, meaning the program name and the window title. There are no screenshots, no keylogging and no screen recording, neither stored nor sent anywhere.

Where does my data go?

Only onto your own device. There is no cloud and no account needed for your tracking data. Nobody but you sees what you did and when.

Does Zeitblick run on Mac?

Right now Zeitblick is built for Windows. A macOS version is on the roadmap once the Windows version is fully polished.

What does it cost, and can I cancel anytime?

Zeitblick costs €10 per year, and the first 7 days are free. At checkout the price is shown in your local currency. You can cancel anytime, your subscription simply won't renew. Your tracked data stays on your device. How to cancel.

How accurate is the tracking?

Zeitblick checks the active window every 5 seconds and automatically detects idle time after 3 minutes without mouse or keyboard input. That's accurate enough for everyday life without drowning you in second-by-second detail.

What if a category doesn't fit?

You can move any program or website to another category with one click. Your change applies immediately and permanently.

My browser or Windows shows a warning. Is that dangerous?

No. Both the download warning ("isn't commonly downloaded") and "Windows protected your PC" appear because the file is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate, not because anything was found. Click "Keep" when downloading, and "More info" and then "Run anyway" when opening it.

Is the app in English?

Yes. Zeitblick is fully available in English and German, you can switch the language at the top of the sidebar.
