Cancel subscription

You can cancel your Zeitblick subscription yourself at any time, no reason needed. It takes about a minute.

Cancel at Link

Link is Stripe's payment service, which you used to subscribe to Zeitblick · no password needed

Go to app.link.com

How to cancel

  1. 1

    Open app.link.com

    Use the button above or the link in the confirmation email you received when you subscribed.

  2. 2

    Sign in with your email

    Use the email address you entered at checkout. Link sends you a verification code, so you don't need a password.

  3. 3

    Cancel the subscription

    Find Zeitblick among your purchases, open it and choose "Cancel subscription". You'll get a confirmation by email.

What happens next

🎁

Within the first 7 days

Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing at all.

📅

After that

Your subscription won't renew and nothing else is charged.

💾

Your data

It only lives on your PC. If you uninstall Zeitblick (Settings → Apps → Zeitblick), it's deleted as well.
